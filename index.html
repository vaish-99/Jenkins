Docker

Builds images and runs containers: the packaged app process.

Kubernetes

Coordinates containers across machines and maintains desired state.

Pod

The smallest Kubernetes unit; usually wraps one app container.

In short: Docker packages the application; Kubernetes deploys and manages its containers.
